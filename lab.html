How the route is found

This is the travelling salesman problem, which is NP-hard. Nearest neighbour is greedy: from where the van is, always drive to the closest stop not yet visited. 2-opt starts from that route and keeps reversing any stretch of it that removes a crossing, until no reversal helps. Held-Karp is dynamic programming over subsets of stops and is exact in O(n² · 2ⁿ). Brute force tries all n! orders and is exact but only practical up to 8 stops.
